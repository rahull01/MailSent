# License Status — PENDING

MailSent has **not yet selected an open-source license**. This status is deliberate and awaits a founder decision. This document records the state so no one assumes otherwise.

> **Note:** This document must be replaced by the final `LICENSE` file before Production Readiness (Phase 30).

## Why no OSS license is selected yet

- The founders have not decided on the licensing model for MailSent.
- Choosing a license is a legal and strategic decision. It must not be made by default, by convenience, or by the act of committing code.
- Until the founders decide, the default legal position applies: no rights are granted beyond those explicitly given.

## Who owns the copyright

- MailSent and its copyright are owned by the **founders** (per the Project Constitution, the founders own the documentation and the product).
- Contributions are made to the project; contributing does not transfer ownership and does not grant or imply a license.

## No contributor may assume licensing

- No engineer, AI agent, or contributor may assume, imply, or declare a license for the project.
- No one may add a `LICENSE` file, a license header, or a license notice.
- No file or package may be marked MIT, Apache, GPL, or any other license.
- If a licensing decision is needed, it is raised for **founder decision** — it is never made by an engineer or agent.

## Decision record

- The founders will record the licensing decision here when one is made.
- Until then, the repository is **"all rights reserved"** by the copyright owner.
